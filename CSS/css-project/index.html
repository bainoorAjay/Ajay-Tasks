All Aboard! Now Sailing from Singapore







An All-New Adventure Is on the Horizon


Embark on an unforgettable 3- or 4-night holiday aboard the all-new Disney Adventure—setting sail from Singapore. From enchanting Character experiences to a spectacular fireworks show lighting up the night sky, magical moments await at every turn. Already booked? Get ready for your cruise by reviewing our dedicated pre-arrival guide.


Maximize Your Fun in 7 Themed Areas

Sail away on a magical adventure as you explore 7 imaginatively themed areas across the ship—

each bursting with immersive experiences that bring the tales from Disney, Marvel and Pixar to life

on a grand scale.

Artful Accommodations

Whether you’re planning a fun-filled family vacation, a friends getaway or a couples escape,,

Disney staterooms and suites ensure your relaxation and comfort. Each accommodation features

magical touches, including artwork inspired by Disney, Pixar or Marvel stories.










Concierge Suites & Staterooms

Elevate your holiday at sea with our lavishly appointed Concierge Staterooms and Suites. As a,

Concierge Guest, you’ll receive exclusive perks like priority boarding, exclusive access to a private

Concierge lounge and sundeck, and the attention of a dedicated team—ensuring you feel.

pampered before and during your voyage.
