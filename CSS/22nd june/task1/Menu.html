# Coffee type Main Ingredients / Preparation Cafe Price Range
1 Espresso 1 oz. concentrated shot of pure coffee extracted under high pressure $3.00 – $4.50
2 Doppio 1–2 shots of espresso + hot water $3.75 – $4.50
3 Americano 1–2 shots of espresso + hot water $3.75 – $4.50
4 Café Latte 1–2 shots of espresso + 6–10 oz. steamed milk + thin layer of microfoam $4.50 – $6.00
5 Cappuccino Equal parts espresso, steamed milk, and thick milk foam (1:1:1 ratio) $4.50 – $5.50
6 Macchiato 1 shot of espresso + a small "stain" or dollop of steamed milk foam $3.50 – $4.50
7 Cortado Equal parts espresso and warm, textured milk (1:1 ratio) $4.00 – $5.00
8 Flat White 2 shots of espresso (often ristretto) + velvety, tightly-integrated microfoam $4.50 – $5.75
9 Mocha 1–2 shots of espresso + chocolate syrup/powder + steamed milk + whipped cream $4.50 – $5.75
10 Affogato 1 scoop of vanilla ice cream or gelato + 1–2 shots of hot espresso poured over $5.50 – $7.00
11 Café au Lait Equal parts strong brewed drip coffee and warm, scalded milk (1:1 ratio) $3.50 – $4.50
12 Cold Brew Coarsely ground coffee steeped in cold water for 12–24 hours + ice $4.50 – $5.50
13 Nitro Cold Brew Cold brew coffee infused with nitrogen gas for a creamy, draft-beer texture $5.00 – $6.50
14 Nitro Cold Brew Hot brewed coffee + Irish whiskey + sugar + topped with thick cream $8.00 – $12.00
15 Ristretto A short, highly concentrated shot of espresso brewed with half the water $3.00 – $4.00
16 Red Eye 6 oz. regular drip-brewed coffee + 1 shot of espresso added $4.50 – $5.50
17 Frappé Instant coffee + water + sugar + ice, shaken or blended until highly frothy $4.50 – $6.00
18 Filter Coffee Dark roasted coffee decoction (with chicory) + boiling milk + sugar $2.50 – $4.00
19 Lungo An espresso shot extracted for longer, using double the water for a looser, more bitter profile $3.00 – $4.50
20 Café Breve 1 shot of espresso + steamed half-and-half (milk & cream blend) + a layer of foam $4.75 – $6.00
